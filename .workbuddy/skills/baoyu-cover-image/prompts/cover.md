# Cover Image Prompt

## Visual Concept
A conceptual illustration showing the contrast between information asymmetry and wealth distribution.

## Key Elements
- **Central metaphor**: A towering mountain peak (information highland) on one side, a deep valley (information lowland) on the other
- **Visual representation**: Silhouettes of people standing at different elevations
- **Symbolism**: Those on the peak see vast landscapes and opportunities, those in the valley see only walls
- **Color scheme**: Deep navy blues, golden accents for wealth, contrasting shadows

## Composition
- **Foreground**: Bold Chinese title "财富是信息不对称决定的"
- **Background**: Abstract representation of data flows, information networks
- **Atmosphere**: Thought-provoking, slightly mysterious, professional

## Style Notes
- Minimalist flat vector design
- Strong contrast between light and shadow
- Clean geometric shapes
- No realistic human faces
